# External anatomy viewer attribution

MediSense embeds the Human Atlas viewer at https://atlas.taim.best/ and does not bundle or modify its anatomy geometry.

- Viewer source: https://github.com/slorksmo/Human-Atlas (MIT application license)
- Male reference data: BodyParts3D 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)
- Female reference data: Human Reference Atlas v1.10 (CC BY 4.0)
- Additional female lower-limb muscle data: Andreassen et al. (2023), CC BY 4.0
- Data attribution and adaptation details: https://atlas.taim.best/ATTRIBUTION.md

The atlas documents that its models have different source coverage. Some female skeleton groups are adapted from the male reference and are labelled in the viewer. This educational resource is not a patient-specific scan or a diagnostic tool. The embedded viewer requires an internet connection and is hosted by its maintainers.
